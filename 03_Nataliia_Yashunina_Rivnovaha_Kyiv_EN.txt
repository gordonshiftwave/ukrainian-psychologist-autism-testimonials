Hello, my name is Nataliia Yashunina. I am a psychologist, and I work at the Rivnovaha (Equilibrium) Psychological Center in Kyiv.

I have a little experience using the Shiftwave chair in my work, specifically in an integrative way. I do neuropsychological correction with children, I do Floortime, and after the session we go and do a session in the chair.

Which children do I work with? Most often these are children diagnosed with ASD (autism spectrum disorder) or ADHD. To describe their symptoms, most often they have difficulty with self-regulation, frustration, tantrums, and take a long time to calm themselves down.

These difficulties come with, and are combined with, differences in how the nervous system works in their sensory profile. Most often I see hyposensitivity of the proprioceptive system, hyper- or hyposensitivity of the vestibular modality, weak postural control, and a weak sense of interoceptive signals, which affect both attention and the quality of contact.

Some of the children we did Shiftwave with had speech delay. These are children aged four and older.

How do children react to the visit and to the chair therapy? Some sit down easily and relax right away. Some children need more time, even several sessions, from one to three, to sit in the chair longer and tolerate these sensations.

Some may refuse the headphones or the eye mask, possibly because of tactile sensitivity to being touched on the head, or sensitivity to sounds.

What I observe: children may place particular parts of their body right on the little circles that the vibration comes from. Children often lift their legs to stand with their feet on the circles, or press their palms to them, and some even wanted to put their cheek on them, further exploring this movement.

The reactions we see most often: children tune in to the sensations. Some may even sort of go still, quiet down a little. Some react with a smile, start laughing, or start moving more. Some may visibly relax, as if sinking back into the chair.

After the session, some children come out collected, regulated, and calm.

I also want to say that some children need only a specialist with them, while some who are more anxious may need a parent present.

Thank you.
