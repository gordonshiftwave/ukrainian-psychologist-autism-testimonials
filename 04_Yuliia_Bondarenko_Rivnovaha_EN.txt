Good afternoon. I am Yuliia Bondarenko, a psychologist at the Rivnovaha (Equilibrium) Psychological Assistance Center.

I want to tell you about my experience working with Shiftwave.

I use the 10-minute program in Ukrainian, adapted for children. I use it with children aged 5 to 9. These are children with insufficient sensitivity of the proprioceptive system, who have a poor sense of their own body.

I run the session before a lesson, with the goal of lowering the level of bodily tension and helping the child better sense their own body.

I use vibration intensity up to 50%, set individually for each child.

After the session, the child becomes calmer and can concentrate better during the lesson.

In my experience, all the children refuse the mask, and some also refuse the headphones, but they respond well to the vibration.

Some children go through the whole 10-minute session right away, while others need to get used to the new sensations gradually. So we gradually increase the session time.
