Good afternoon. My name is Olha Rozhyna. I am the head of the correctional groups at the school of the Rivnovaha (Equilibrium) Center.

Children aged 6 to 12 study at our school. Most of our pupils have autism spectrum disorders.

In our work with children, we use the Shiftwave chair as an additional means of supporting sensory regulation.

We usually use the 10-minute program with Ukrainian translation, choosing the mode according to the individual needs of the child.

Based on our observations, after using the chair, children show positive changes in their learning activities. We see improved concentration and attention, an improved ability to sustain attention for a certain period of time, and improved interaction with the specialist. Children become more ready to take in instructions and to carry out both instructions and tasks.

For children with heightened motor agitation, we use the chair as an additional means of relaxation, and here we use vibration in a lower range.

For children with reduced activity, we also use the chair, but the vibration range is higher, above 50.

In our practice, the chair is an additional tool for supporting children with special educational needs. We use it as one of the possible components of comprehensive support, combined with correctional and developmental sessions.
